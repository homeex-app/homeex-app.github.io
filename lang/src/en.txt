@1
Bridging
Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat on the floor. Lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from your shoulders to your knees, squeezing your buttocks. Hold for a few seconds, then lower your hips slowly.
@2
Plank
Get into a push-up–like position, resting on your forearms and the tips of your toes. Keep your back straight and your whole body in one straight line.
@3
Bridging with band
Wrap a resistance band around your knees before you start. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat on the floor. Slowly lift your hips until your body forms a straight line from your knees to your shoulders. As you lift, push your knees slightly outward against the band to work the outer thigh muscles as well as the buttocks, then lower slowly.
@4
One leg bridging
Lie on your back with one knee bent and that foot on the floor, and keep the other leg straight in the air. Lift your hips using only the leg on the floor, keeping the raised leg straight and your hips level without tilting, then lower slowly.
@5
Knee to chest
Lie on your back and pull one knee toward your chest with your hands until you feel a gentle stretch in your lower back and buttocks. Hold for 15 to 30 seconds, then return the leg and repeat with the other leg.
@6
Isometric abdominal
Lie on your back with your knees bent. Draw your lower belly in, as if bringing your belly button toward your spine, without holding your breath. Hold this for a few seconds while breathing normally, then relax.
@7
Anterior pelvic tilt (lying on your back)
Lie on your back with your legs straight or bent, whichever is more comfortable. Gently tilt your pelvis forward so the arch of your lower back lifts slightly off the floor, without lifting your buttocks. Hold for a few seconds, then return to normal. This is a small movement and should not cause any pain.
@8
Transversus abdominis activation
Lie on your back with your knees bent. Draw your lower belly in so the tightening is only in the deepest layer of your abdominal muscles, without tensing the outer belly muscles or holding your breath. Hold this position while breathing normally.
@9
Cross-body oblique activation
Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet on the floor. Place your palm on the outside of the opposite knee. Push your hand against the knee while pushing the knee against your hand with equal force, in opposite directions, so that nothing actually moves. Keep this pressure for 5 to 8 seconds, focusing on tightening the muscles on the side of your belly, then release slowly and with control. Repeat the required number of times before switching sides.
@10
Hip adduction
Lie on your back with your knees bent and place a small ball or pillow between your knees. Squeeze the ball or pillow with your knees to tighten your inner thigh muscles. Hold for a few seconds, then relax.
@11
Straight leg raise (SLR)
Lie on your back with one knee bent and that foot on the floor, and the other leg fully straight. Tighten the muscle at the front of the thigh of the straight leg, then lift it to the height of the bent knee without bending it, and lower it slowly.
@12
Wall hamstring stretch
Lie on your back in a doorway and rest one leg straight up against the wall as far as you can (if the muscle is tight, the leg will be at less than a right angle), while the other leg lies straight on the floor through the doorway. This stretches the back of the thigh and eases lower-back tightness while keeping your spine straight and supported.
@13
Piriformis stretch
Lie on your back and place the ankle of the affected leg over the knee of the other leg, making a figure 4. Hold the back of the other thigh and pull it toward your chest until you feel a stretch deep in the buttock on the affected side. Hold for 15 to 30 seconds.
@14
Serratus punch
Lie on your back holding a light weight or a band, with your arm straight up toward the ceiling. At the end of the movement, push your arm a little higher by lifting your shoulder blade slightly off the floor, then lower it slowly.
@15
Quadriceps set
Lie down or sit with your leg fully straight. Press the back of your knee down into the floor as if tightening the muscle at the front of your thigh, without moving the leg. Hold for a few seconds, then relax.
@16
McKenzie extension (cobra)
Lie on your stomach with your hands under your shoulders. Push with your hands to lift your upper body while your hips and thighs stay on the floor, then slowly return to the starting position. Stop at the first feeling of pain and do not continue the movement past it.
@17
Glutes activation 1
Lie on your stomach and squeeze both buttocks together for a few seconds without moving your legs or back, then relax.
@18
Glutes activation 2
Lie on your stomach and squeeze both buttocks together for a few seconds without moving your legs or back, then relax.
@19
Back extension (lying on your stomach)
Lie on your stomach with your arms by your sides. Lift your chest and upper body off the floor using only your lower-back muscles, without pushing with your arms. Hold for a few seconds, then lower slowly.
@20
Scapular W raises
Lie on your stomach and bring your bent elbows close to your sides so your arms and body form a W. Lift your arms slightly off the floor while squeezing your shoulder blades back and down. Hold for 2 seconds, then lower slowly.
@21
Scapular T raises
Lie on your stomach and stretch your arms straight out to the sides at shoulder level to form a T. Lift your arms by squeezing your shoulder blades toward your spine, then lower them with control.
@22
Scapular V raises
Lie on your stomach and stretch your arms forward and out at an angle to form a V, thumbs pointing up toward the ceiling. Lift your arms slightly using your upper-back and shoulder-blade muscles, then lower them slowly.
@23
Hip abduction
Lie on your side with both legs straight. Lift the top leg up, keeping it fully straight and without letting it drift forward or back, then lower it slowly.
@24
Clam shell
Lie on your side with your knees bent to about 45 degrees and your heels together. Open the top knee upward like a clam shell, without moving your pelvis or rolling it back, then lower it slowly.
@25
Spinal derotation positioning
Lie on your side with your hips and knees bent to about 90 degrees and your knees together. Keep your lower body still and turn your upper trunk and top arm back toward the floor behind you, following your hand with your eyes, until you feel a gentle stretch in your spine. Return slowly.
@26
Serratus wall slide
Stand facing the wall and place your forearms vertically on the wall, with a foam roller or resistance band between your hands. Slowly slide your forearms up and down the wall, pressing gently forward and pushing your shoulder blades outward to target the muscle.
@27
Sleeper stretch
Lie on your affected side with that shoulder directly under you. Bend the elbow to 90 degrees with the forearm in front of you, then use your other hand to gently push the forearm toward the floor until you feel a stretch at the back of the shoulder.
@28
Mini squat
Stand in front of a stable chair with your feet shoulder-width apart. Slowly lower yourself as if to sit, about halfway down or until you lightly touch the chair, then push back up with your legs. Do not let your knees fall inward.
@29
Step up
Stand in front of a stable step or platform. Step up fully with the leg you want to strengthen first, then bring up the other leg. Step down in the same order, so the affected leg comes down first.
@30
Heel raises
Stand holding something stable for balance. Rise up onto your toes, hold for 1 second, then lower slowly.
@31
Hip flexor stretch
Take a step forward into a lunge with your back knee on the floor. Push your hips forward while keeping your body upright until you feel a stretch at the front of the thigh of the back leg. Hold for 15 to 30 seconds.
@32
Calf stretch
Stand on the edge of a stair on the front of your foot only, with your heel off the step, holding something stable for balance. Lower your heel below the level of the step until you feel a stretch at the back of your lower leg. Hold, then come back up.
@33
Weight shift
Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart. Slowly shift your body weight from one leg to the other, side to side, without lifting your feet. Then repeat shifting forward and back, to improve balance and body control.
@34
Codman's exercises
Stand holding something stable with your good hand and lean forward slightly, letting the affected arm hang down loosely. Gently swing the arm in small circles or forward and back, letting the movement come from your body rocking, not from using your shoulder muscles.
@35
Biceps curl
Hold a band or a light weight with your elbow fixed at your side. Bend your forearm up toward your shoulder, then lower it slowly.
@36
Shoulder external rotation with band
Stand beside the band's anchor point and bend the elbow farther from it to 90 degrees, keeping it against your side. Hold the band and rotate your forearm up and out, away from your belly, keeping your elbow against your body the whole time. Hold for 1 second at the end, then slowly return.
@37
Shoulder internal rotation with band
Stand beside the band's anchor point and bend the elbow closer to it to 90 degrees, keeping it against your side. Hold the band and rotate your forearm inward toward your belly, keeping your elbow in place. Hold for 1 second at the end, then slowly return outward toward the anchor point.
@38
Serratus push-up
Get into a push-up position, on your knees or on your feet, and do a normal push-up. At the top, push a little further so your chest moves farther from the floor and your shoulder blades spread apart from your spine.
@39
Lower trap with band
Stand on the band and hold both ends. Raise your arm up and out to the side at an angle, in a Y shape, while drawing your shoulder blade down and back.
@40
Wall slide
Stand with your back against the wall, arms bent and touching the wall in roughly a W shape. Slide your arms up over your head, trying to keep them touching the wall as much as possible, then slowly return to the start.
@41
De Quervain stretch
Stretch your arm out in front of you and tuck your thumb into your palm, closing your other fingers over it. Slowly bend your wrist toward the little finger until you feel a gentle stretch on the thumb side of the wrist. Hold for 15 to 30 seconds.
@42
Wrist flexors stretch
Stretch your arm out in front of you with the palm facing up. With your other hand, pull your fingers down and back until you feel a stretch on the inside of the forearm. Hold for 15 to 30 seconds.
@43
Wrist extensors stretch
Stretch your arm out in front of you with the palm facing down. With your other hand, pull the back of your hand down and toward you until you feel a stretch on the top of the forearm. Hold for 15 to 30 seconds.
@44
Ankle dorsiflexion with band
Sit with your legs straight. Wrap a resistance band around the front of your foot with the other end anchored, then pull the front of your foot up toward you against the band.
@45
Ankle eversion with band
Sit in the same position and wrap the band around the outer edge of your foot, anchored to the side. Turn your foot outward against the resistance.
@46
Ankle inversion with band
Sit in the same position and wrap the band around the inner edge of your foot. Turn your foot inward against the resistance.
@47
Ankle pump
Sit or lie down with your legs straight. Move your foot up and down at the ankle several times, then make full circles with your foot in both directions.
@48
Upper trap stretch
Sit up straight and hold the edge of the chair seat with the hand on the side you want to stretch. Tilt your head to the other side while keeping that shoulder down, until you feel a stretch along the side of your neck and shoulder.
@49
Levator scapulae stretch
Sit up straight and tilt your head down and to the side at about 45 degrees, as if looking into your armpit. You can help by placing your hand on top of your head and pushing gently, until you feel a stretch at the back of your neck and shoulder.
@50
Isometric neck
Sit and place your hand on your forehead, then push your head against your hand without actually moving your head. Repeat the same from the back and from both sides.
@54
Shoulder shrugs
Sit up straight and lift your shoulders up toward your ears until you feel them tighten. Hold for 2 seconds, then lower slowly.
@55
Curl up (abdominal)
Lie on your back with your knees bent and your arms raised. Breathing out, slowly lift your head and shoulders until only your shoulder blades come off the floor, tightening your upper belly. Hold for a moment, then lower slowly with control while breathing in, without lifting your lower back off the floor.
@56
Pectoralis major self-stretch
Stand in a corner of the room with your forearms on the two walls, then gently lean your chest forward until you feel a comfortable stretch across your chest.
@57
Hip extension with pool noodle
Stand on one leg in the pool and place the other foot on top of a pool noodle, pressing it down. Slowly push the noodle back and down against the water's resistance to strengthen your buttocks and lower back, keeping your body straight.
@58
Multifidus activation
Get on your hands and knees, with your hands directly under your shoulders and your knees under your hips. Keep your spine and pelvis in a neutral position without arching your back. Lift one knee just a few centimetres off the floor without any twisting or tilting of your pelvis. Hold for 1 second and lower the knee slowly, then switch sides, keeping your spine as steady as possible.
@59
Bird dog (opposite arm & leg raise on all fours)
Get on your hands and knees, hands under your shoulders and knees under your hips. Stretch out your right arm and left leg at the same time, keeping your back steady without twisting your pelvis, then return and switch sides.
Dose: 10 times each side, hold 5 seconds.
@61
Child's pose (kneeling back stretch)
Sit back on your heels with your knees slightly apart, lean your body forward and stretch your arms out on the floor in front of you. Let your head rest on the floor or a pillow until you feel a gentle stretch in your lower back.
Dose: 3 times, hold 30 seconds.
@62
Tandem stance (heel-to-toe standing balance)
Stand next to a wall or chair for safety. Place the heel of one foot right against the toes of the other, as if standing on a straight line, and keep your balance. When it gets easy, try without holding on, and then with your eyes closed.
Dose: 3 times with each leg in front, hold 30 seconds.
@63
Chin tuck (sitting)
Sit on a chair with your back straight, looking forward. Draw your chin straight back as if making a double chin, without tipping your head down or up, until you feel a gentle stretch at the back of your neck. Hold, then return slowly.
Dose: 2 sets of 10 reps, hold 5 seconds, twice a day.
@64
Neck rotation (turn head right & left)
Sit with your back straight and your shoulders relaxed. Slowly turn your head to the right as if looking over your shoulder, as far as comfortable without pain, then return to the middle and repeat to the left. Keep your chin level during the movement.
Dose: 10 times each way, twice a day.
@65
Neck side bending (ear to shoulder)
Sit looking straight ahead. Tilt your head to the side to bring your right ear toward your right shoulder, without lifting the shoulder or turning your face, until you feel a gentle stretch on the opposite side of your neck. Hold, then return and repeat on the other side.
Dose: 5 times each side, hold 15 seconds.
@66
Deep neck flexors – head nod lying on back
Lie on your back with your knees bent and a small folded towel under your head. Make a very small nod as if saying "yes", bringing your chin toward your neck, without lifting your head off the towel and without tensing the muscles at the front of your neck. Hold while breathing normally, then relax.
Dose: 10 times, hold 10 seconds, once a day.
@67
Shoulder flexion with stick (lying on back)
Lie on your back with your knees bent, holding a stick with both hands shoulder-width apart, palms down. Slowly raise the stick forward and then over your head, using your good arm to help the affected arm, until you feel a gentle stretch in the shoulder. Lower it slowly.
Dose: 2 sets of 10 reps, twice a day.
@68
Shoulder external rotation with stick (lying on back)
Lie on your back with the elbow of the affected arm against your body and bent to 90 degrees, with a small towel under the elbow. Hold a stick with both hands and use your good arm to push the hand of the affected arm outward, away from your belly, keeping the elbow against your body, until you feel a gentle stretch. Return slowly.
Dose: 2 sets of 10 reps, hold 5 seconds.
@69
Finger wall walk (shoulder elevation)
Stand facing the wall, a small step away, and place the fingertips of the affected hand on the wall at chest level. Walk your fingers up the wall step by step to the highest comfortable point without hiking your shoulder toward your ear, then walk them back down slowly.
Dose: 10 times, twice a day.
@70
Shoulder blade squeeze (scapular retraction)
Sit or stand with your back straight and your arms by your sides. Squeeze your shoulder blades back and down as if holding a pencil between them, without lifting your shoulders or arching your lower back. Hold, then relax slowly.
Dose: 2 sets of 10 reps, hold 5 seconds.
@71
Posterior shoulder stretch (arm across chest)
Stand or sit and bring the affected arm across your chest toward the other side. Use your other hand to gently pull the arm, above the elbow, toward your chest until you feel a stretch at the back of the shoulder. Keep the shoulder down and don't twist your trunk.
Dose: 3 times, hold 30 seconds.
@72
Elbow bending & straightening (active ROM)
Sit with your arm by your side and your palm facing forward. Slowly bend your elbow to bring your hand toward your shoulder as far as comfortable, then straighten the elbow fully.
Dose: 2 sets of 15 reps, twice a day.
@73
Forearm rotation – palm up / palm down
Sit with your elbow against your body, bent to 90 degrees, thumb pointing up. Slowly turn your forearm so your palm faces up, then turn it so your palm faces down, keeping your elbow against your body. For more resistance, hold a light hammer or a water bottle by its end.
Dose: 2 sets of 15 reps.
@74
Eccentric wrist extensors with weight (tennis elbow)
Sit with your forearm resting on a table, wrist over the edge and palm down, holding a light weight or a water bottle. Lift the wrist up with the help of your other hand, then let it lower on its own slowly over 3 to 5 seconds. Mild pain during the exercise is acceptable; stop if the pain becomes sharp.
Dose: 3 sets of 15 reps, once a day.
